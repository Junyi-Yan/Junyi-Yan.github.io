About me

Junyi Yan is a PhD student in National University of Defense Technology (NUDT) advised by Prof. Xinwang Liu. Her research interests include Graph Learning, Graph Anomaly Detection and Deep Graph Clustering. Besides, she also explores related areas such as Graph Foundation Models, LLM for Graph Anomaly Detection, LLM Hallucination Detection, Federated Learning and Time Series.
